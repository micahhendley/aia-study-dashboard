# AIA Study Dashboard

A study dashboard you can open in any browser and make your own with AI. Built for the Artificial Intelligence Association (AIA) at California Baptist University.

**Try it live:** https://micahhendley.github.io/aia-study-dashboard/

## What it does

- **What are you studying for?** Type a topic to get quick searches on Quizlet, YouTube and Khan Academy, plus a "Quiz me in ChatGPT" button. Drop in PDFs, slides and notes, or save links to Quizlet sets, textbooks and Google Docs. PDFs and images open right on the page.
- **Study Timer:** 25 minutes by default. Change the length with the − and + buttons or type your own.
- **To-Do List:** Check off tasks to cross them out and move them to the bottom. Delete any task with the trash icon.
- **Study Playlist:** Paste a Spotify or Apple Music link to play it on the page.
- **Light and dark mode:** Switch at the top right.

## Get started

1. Click the green **Code** button above, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Unzip it and double-click `index.html`. It opens in your browser.

That's it. Everything lives in that one file: the layout (HTML), the look (CSS) and the behavior (JavaScript).

## Make it yours with AI

1. Open `index.html` in a text editor (TextEdit, Notepad or VS Code) and copy everything.
2. Paste it into ChatGPT and ask for a change, for example:
   - "Add a 5-minute break timer that starts when the study timer ends."
   - "Change the accent color to CBU gold."
   - "Save my to-do list so it's still there after I refresh."
3. Ask ChatGPT for **only the parts that changed**, not the whole file. Long files can get cut off.
4. Paste the changes into your file, save it and refresh the browser.

Each section in the file is labeled (`STUDY TIMER`, `TO-DO LIST` and so on), so you can tell ChatGPT exactly where to look.

## Good to know

- **Files you add stay on your computer.** Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Files, tasks and links clear when you refresh the page.
- **Hearing 30-second song previews?** Sign in to Spotify or Apple Music in the same browser, then reload the player. Full Apple Music songs need a subscription.
